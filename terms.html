Joker Pro terms

Updated 13 November 2026

Joker Chess is free to play: every game, the rating, the bots, the Road, the puzzles and the review of your moves. Joker Pro is a paid plan on top of it. It is sold by Kozyra Media Group FZCO, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, on joker-chess.com. These terms say what you buy and how it works.

What Joker Pro gives

  • The Joker analysis in the review of every game, and hints in games against the bots without a daily limit.
  • The Pro puzzle pack and its marathon.
  • 50% more XP, every season of the Road open at once, and streak freezes (twice a month a streak that ran out after a break of up to a week comes back).
  • A gold name with the Pro mark, visible to other players, and looks only Pro has.

We may add to this list. If we ever take something out of it, we will say so here before it happens.

Prices are in US dollars. Your bank may convert the amount and add its own fee. Taxes are added where the law requires it; the payment page shows the final amount before you pay.

Paying

Payments are handled by Stripe on its own page. We never see or keep your card number. Pro is tied to your Joker Chess account, so you need a saved account (an email or Google sign-in) to buy it, and it works on every device where you sign in to that account, including the iPhone and Android apps.

Free trial

The yearly plan starts with 7 free days. You enter a card when you start it. If you cancel before the 7 days are over, nothing is charged. Otherwise the first year is charged when the trial ends. Each account gets the trial once.

Renewal and cancelling

A monthly or yearly plan renews by itself at the end of each period at the price shown above. You can cancel at any time in Profile → Joker Pro → Manage. After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for and is not charged again. If a renewal payment does not go through, Pro stays on for three more days while Stripe tries again, then turns off; nothing you earned in the game is lost.

To delete your Joker Chess account while a plan is running, cancel the plan first.

Refunds

If something went wrong with a payment, write to us and we will sort it out. A Founder payment is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of paying; Pro then turns off. A subscription period that has started is not refunded in part, unless the law where you live gives you that right: if it does, that right is yours and nothing here takes it away.

Fair use

Pro is for one person's account. We may end Pro without a refund on an account used to cheat in rated games or to abuse other players.

Changes

If the price of a plan changes, it changes for new purchases. For a plan that is already running we will tell you by email at least 30 days before a renewal at a new price, and you can cancel before it.

Contact

Questions about Joker Pro or a payment: u.kazyra@kozyramedia.com. How we handle your data: the privacy policy.

Пробный период

План на год начинается с 7 бесплатных дней. Карту нужно указать сразу. Если отменить до конца этих 7 дней, ничего не спишется. Иначе первый год оплачивается, когда пробный период заканчивается. Пробный период даётся на аккаунт один раз.

План на месяц или на год продлевается сам в конце каждого периода по цене из таблицы. Отменить можно в любой момент: Профиль → Joker Pro → Управлять. После отмены Pro работает до конца оплаченного периода, и больше ничего не списывается. Если платёж за продление не прошёл, Pro остаётся ещё на три дня, пока Stripe пробует снова, потом отключается; всё заработанное в игре остаётся.

Чтобы удалить аккаунт Joker Chess при действующем плане, сначала отмените план.
